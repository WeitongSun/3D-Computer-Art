# Sound

*1 person · needs the Thing World v1.1 update · [all roles](README.md)*

**Your job:** Make every sound file of the city.

## How

1. **Background** = heard everywhere (music, city hum). Only one.
2. **Ambient** = heard near one spot, louder as you walk closer (fountain, wind, birds). Ask classmates what their pieces need.
3. **Effects** = door creak, beep, pickup chime. You make the file; Interaction connects it. Check the Miro SOUND ORDERS board.
4. Put the clip in `Assets/Creators/YourName/Sounds`, click it → **World Building → Sound: Make Ambient Sound** or **Make Background Sound**.
5. Mark where each ambient sound goes on the Miro CITY MAP.
6. Use .wav / .ogg / .mp3. Loops should start and end smoothly.

## Limits

1 background sound · ≤ 8 looping sounds

## Work with

Interaction (effects), Landscape, every classmate whose piece needs a sound
