# Textures

*1–2 people · needs the Thing World v1.1 update · [all roles](README.md)*

**Your job:** Make sure the final city doesn't look strange — without making everyone look the same.

## How

1. With Landscape: make the ground materials (roads, grass, plazas) — the base every piece stands on.
2. Visit each classmate's piece in their World Test Scene. Check: texture scale (is a brick 20 cm?), brightness, how it meets the ground.
3. Suggest fixes; everyone keeps their own style.
4. If you make a material for others: **World Building → Shared: Create Shared Material**, then **Shared: Export My Shared Library** → hard drive `02_Shared`.

## Limits

textures ≤ 2048 px · URP/Lit only

## Work with

Landscape first, then every asset maker one by one

## Checklist

- [ ] First ground materials made with Landscape
- [ ] Visited everyone
- [ ] Final materials exported

## Common questions

**How do others use my materials?**
Export your library (Shared: Export My Shared Library), put it in `02_Shared` on the hard drive. Others double-click it → Import, then drag the material onto their model.

**I updated a material — do people need to re-import?**
Yes, export and they import again. Everything using it updates automatically.

**A texture looks blurry or too big.**
Change **Tiling** on the material (e.g. 4 × 4 for a large wall). Check the size: is a brick about 20 cm?

**Do I make everyone use the same materials?**
No. Everyone keeps their own style. You help their pieces sit well in the city: texture scale, brightness, where they meet the ground.
